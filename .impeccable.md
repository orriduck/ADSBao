## Design Context

This is a concise product brief. `DESIGN.md` is the visual implementation
source of truth; `PRODUCT.md` owns the product direction; `CLAUDE.md` owns
validation. When they conflict with a generic design skill, the project
documents win.

### Users

ADSBao is for aviation-focused operators, pilots, spotters, and technically
fluent enthusiasts reading an airport's live operating picture. The map stays
primary while weather, traffic, route intent, and selected-aircraft detail are
scanned quickly.

### Brand Personality

Industrial, precise, cinematic, and calm under load. It should feel like a
serious field-operations console—not generic SaaS, consumer travel polish, or
military simulation.

### Active visual system

- Fixed/in-flow sidebars are operational wayfinding: square joined modules,
  36px icon rails, flat white light surfaces and flat black dark surfaces, and
  single neutral dividers.
- Orange is a scarce primary tracking signal; blue identifies secondary or
  alternate-unit interaction; neutral gray carries ordinary structure.
- Glass is not a general card material. Reuse existing frosted tokens only for
  floating map toolbars, menus, popovers, sheets, and dialogs.
- Use typography, alignment, and data density for hierarchy. Avoid rounded
  marketing cards, decorative gradients, shadows, glow, and unnecessary motion.
- Motion is short, state-driven, opacity/transform only, and respects reduced
  motion.

Before UI implementation, read `DESIGN.md` and inspect the affected screen in
a real browser in the relevant theme. Preserve active routes, click paths, and
map-layer semantics unless the task explicitly changes them.
